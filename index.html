About Our College

Main Entrance Gate · IMCG Korang Town

A premier educational institution offering comprehensive School (Class 1–10) and College (1st & 2nd Year) education in Morning & Evening shifts.

Islamabad Model College for Girls, Korang Town is a prestigious composite institution under the Federal Directorate of Education (FDE). It delivers foundational schooling from Class 1 to 10 (School Section) as well as higher secondary 1st and 2nd Year (College Section), functioning in both Morning and Evening shifts to accommodate a vast student community.

This website serves as a student-curated gateway to highlight college highlights, academic disciplines, campus facilities, and student accomplishments.

Information Transparency: Official administration circulars, faculty lists, and institutional updates are coordinated with college guidelines.

Admissions & Guidelines

Centralized admission guidelines and eligibility criteria for HSSC Part-I (Intermediate) under the Federal Directorate of Education (FDE), Islamabad.

📊 Eligibility & Programs

Admissions are granted strictly on merit according to FDE policy guidelines following Matriculation results.

Discipline Required Subjects Min. Merit
F.Sc Pre-Medical Biology, Chemistry, Physics 65% (Matric)
F.Sc Pre-Engineering Maths, Physics, Chemistry 65% (Matric)
ICS (Comp. Science) Comp. Sci, Maths, Physics/Stats 60% (Matric)
Humanities / Arts General Electives & Languages 50% (Matric)
Required Documents Checklist:
✓ Matric Result Card (4 copies)
✓ Character Certificate
✓ Student B-Form / CNIC
✓ Father/Guardian CNIC
✓ 4 Passport Photos (Blue BG)
✓ FDE Online Form Copy

📝 How to Apply (Step-by-Step)

Follow these streamlined steps to complete your admission process smoothly:

1
Online Registration

Visit the official FDE online portal and register an account using your Matric roll number and B-Form.

2
Select IMCG Korang Town

Choose Islamabad Model College for Girls, Korang Town as your preferred college and select your desired discipline.

3
Merit List Display

Check the merit lists published on the college noticeboard and official FDE website on scheduled notification dates.

4
Document Verification & Fee

Bring original credentials to the college admission desk, verify documents, and deposit prescribed institutional dues.

🔔 Important Admission Note:

Applicants applying on quotas (FDE Employees, Sports, Disabled, or Defense Personnel) must attach authorized verified certificates with their application form.

COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about admissions, shifts, uniforms, bus routes, and campus life at IMCG Korang Town.

How do admissions for Morning and Evening shifts work? +
Admissions for both Morning (8:00 AM – 1:00 PM) and Evening (1:00 PM – 5:30 PM) shifts are processed through the centralized Federal Directorate of Education (FDE) portal. Each shift has its own separate merit list based on Matriculation scores.
What are the exact uniform guidelines for school vs. college? +
For Morning Shift: School students (Class 1–10) wear Sky Blue Kameez with white shalwar; College students (1st & 2nd Year) wear Full White.
For Evening Shift: School students wear Navy Blue Kameez with white shalwar; College students wear Full White.
Is college bus transport available for all students? +
Yes! IMCG Korang Town operates designated bus transport covering Korang Town, PWD Housing Society, Media Town, Soan Gardens, Police Foundation, CBR Town, and Islamabad Highway for registered students.
How are students assigned to Peace House and Unity House? +
Upon enrollment, students in School and College are assigned to either Peace House (Yellow) or Unity House (Red). Students earn points for their house through academic excellence, sports gala, speech debates, and arts exhibitions.
Can a student change their academic discipline (e.g. Pre-Med to ICS)? +
Yes, discipline changes are allowed within the official change window announced by the Federal Board (FBISE) and college administration, subject to seat availability and meeting the required merit percentage.
What documents must be brought for admission verification? +
Candidates must bring: Original Matric Result Card, School Character Certificate, Student's B-Form / CNIC, Father's or Guardian's CNIC copy, 4 blue-background passport photographs, and the printed FDE online application form.
